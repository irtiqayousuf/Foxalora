FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Foxalora really free?

Yes. Foxalora is free and open source. There are no licence fees and no limits on the number of mailboxes or domains.

Do I need to change my mail servers?

No. Foxalora is only an email client. It connects to the IMAP and SMTP servers you already run, such as Dovecot, Postfix or Exim.

Does Foxalora store my customers' emails?

No. Emails stay on your mail servers. Foxalora only keeps small things like preferences, contacts and settings.

Does it work with cPanel, Plesk and DirectAdmin?

Yes. Foxalora works alongside these panels. Mailboxes, passwords and storage are still managed in your panel, and a cPanel connection is part of the first release.

How do my customers sign in?

With the email address and password they already use. There's no separate account to create.

What do I need to install it?

A Linux server with Docker, and Nginx for HTTPS. The setup wizard handles the rest.

Is it secure?

Security is built in from the start: encrypted connections, no stored plain-text passwords, protection against harmful emails, and OWASP ASVS Level 2 as the target. Read more about security.
