Features

Everything in Foxalora.

A complete webmail for your customers, and a simple control centre for you.

MailWritingFiles & searchContactsPersonaliseAdmin

Mail

A fast, clear inbox that works with any IMAP server.

  • All standard foldersInbox, Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash and Archive, detected from your server.
  • Custom foldersCreate, delete and move mail between your own folders.
  • Unread counts on foldersSee at a glance where new mail is waiting.
  • Stars & read statusStar important mail and mark messages read or unread.
  • Bulk actionsSelect many messages to move, archive, delete or mark.
  • ThreadsReplies are grouped into one tidy conversation.
  • Fast loadingLoads 50 messages at a time and more as you scroll.
  • Reading viewSender, recipients, Cc, attachments, previous and next, print, and open in a new window.

Writing

A composer that's easy for everyone.

  • Rich text editorBold, italic, underline, lists, quotes, links, alignment and images.
  • Plain text modeSwitch to plain text whenever you need it.
  • To, Cc and BccMany recipients, with suggestions from your contacts.
  • Reply, reply all, forwardOriginal message quoted neatly.
  • Drafts saved automaticallyNever lose an unfinished email.
  • Choose your sender addressSend from the right identity every time.
  • SignaturesRich signatures with logos, added automatically.
  • Unsaved-work warningA reminder before leaving a half-written email.

Files & search

Find anything, open everything safely.

  • Drag-and-drop attachmentsAdd many files at once, with sizes shown.
  • Image and PDF previewView files without downloading them.
  • Size limitsClear messages when a file is too large.
  • Remove before sendingTake a file off with one click.
  • Search by anythingSender, recipient, subject, words, date range and attachments.
  • Search everywhereOne folder or all folders, plus unread and starred filters.

Contacts

An address book that fills itself in.

  • Full contact detailsName, company, phone and several email addresses.
  • Import & exportCSV and vCard import, vCard export.
  • Smart suggestionsRecipients suggested as you type.
  • Frequent contactsThe people you email most, always close by.

Personalise

Every user can make it feel like theirs.

  • Light, dark or system themeEasy on the eyes, day or night.
  • Compact or comfortableChoose how much fits on screen.
  • Language & regionLanguage, time zone and date format, with Arabic right-to-left.
  • Reply format & previewsFormatted or plain replies, message previews on or off.
  • Image privacyAsk before loading outside images, or always show them.
  • Notifications & accessibilityNew-mail alerts, keyboard shortcuts and an accessibility menu.

Admin

Simple controls for hosting companies.

  • BrandingApp name, light and dark logos, brand colour.
  • Sign-in pageHeading, message and support email, with a live desktop preview.
  • Mail serversDefault IMAP and SMTP, plus domain-to-server mapping.
  • Security policiesSession timeout, sign-in attempts, encryption and image blocking.
  • Allowed domainsLimit who can sign in.
  • Control-panel linkQuick access to cPanel, Plesk or DirectAdmin for managing mailboxes.
